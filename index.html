01 — About LAXE

Creativity that changes perception.

Est.
beauty
house

LAXE is a creative house for the beauty industry — part propaganda studio, part advertising agency, part brand owner.

We conceive cosmetic brands, give them a voice, and build the campaigns that carry them into culture. Strategists, art directors, writers and image-makers work as one room — from the first naming session to the last frame of a launch film.

Every decision is made for the same reason: to change the way a product is seen, and the way it is felt.

“Perception is the product. Everything else is packaging.”

— The LAXE manifesto
i.

Propaganda & Advertising

Campaigns engineered for attention and memory — film, print, out-of-home and social.

ii.

Cosmetic Brand Creation

We build and own beauty houses, from formula brief and naming to packaging and shelf.

iii.

Visual Experiences

Photography, motion and digital worlds made with the craft of an editorial studio.

03 — Services

Everything a beauty brand needs to be desired.

Ten disciplines, one creative direction. Open any service to see what we deliver.

  • Integrated launch and seasonal campaigns built around one unforgettable idea — scaled from a hero film to every screen, page and shelf.

    • Campaign concept & scripts
    • Media-ready key visuals
    • Out-of-home & print

  • We find the truth a brand can own, then turn it into positioning, architecture and a voice that sets the rules for everything that follows.

    • Audience & category insight
    • Positioning & brand platform
    • Naming & verbal identity

  • One point of view across casting, set, light, type and motion — so every touchpoint feels unmistakably like the same house.

    • Creative vision & mood worlds
    • Art direction on set
    • Brand guardianship

  • Identities for fragrance, skincare and makeup lines — wordmarks, monograms, color and material systems made for glass, metal and paper.

    • Logo & monogram design
    • Color & material language
    • Brand guidelines

  • Bottles, caps, cartons and gift sets that feel precious in the hand — from structural concept to finishes, foils and production files.

    • Structural & secondary packaging
    • Finishes, foils & embossing
    • Print-ready artwork

  • Full-funnel programs that keep luxury codes intact while driving discovery, trial and loyalty across search, paid social and email.

    • Paid media creative
    • Launch funnels & CRM
    • Measurement frameworks

  • Culture-first social stories, creator collaborations and launch moments designed to be saved, shared and talked about.

    • Always-on content systems
    • Creator & talent partnerships
    • Launch-day choreography

  • Still life and beauty photography with sculpted light, liquid textures and the precision luxury packaging demands.

    • Hero & e-commerce stills
    • Texture & swatch imagery
    • Beauty portraiture

  • Launch films, product macros and motion design — produced end-to-end, from treatment and casting to grade and final mix.

    • Hero films & cut-downs

    • Post-production & grade
